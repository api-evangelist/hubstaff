What is it? The Hubstaff Model Context Protocol (MCP) server allows AI assistants and other MCP-compatible clients to access and read data from the Hubstaff workspace securely. Once connected, you can ask questions about your organization, team members, projects, and time or activity data, and receive responses based on your current Hubstaff information.
