When you sign in from a browser or device Hubstaff doesn’t recognize, we send an OTP (one-time password) consisting of a 6-digit code to your email address to confirm it’s really you. Enter the code, and you’re in; no extra setup required. ℹ️ This applies to members who don’t have two-factor authentication enabled.
